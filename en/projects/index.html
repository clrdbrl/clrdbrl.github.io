  • Groupe APB2026

    Groupe APB is a French RGE-certified company specialising in energy renovation for homeowners, from insulation and heating to solar panels, ventilation and joinery. The project involved redesigning the brand identity to strengthen its awareness and harmonise its presence across every channel, from the website to social media and marketing campaigns. The goal was also to simplify offer management, improve the user experience and optimise the customer journey, from acquisition to loyalty, while ensuring continuity between physical and digital touchpoints.

  • NYRO2025

    NYRO was born from a simple belief, beauty should be revealed, not masked. The project brings to life a natural skincare brand, healthy and effective, suited to every skin type and tone, with formulas grounded in dermatological research and clinically proven efficacy. The visual identity expresses this standard through a clean, luminous universe. White and blue inspire trust, violet evokes the gentleness of care and red adds warmth. At the crossroads of medical rigour and sensitivity, it embodies an inclusive vision of natural beauty.

  • Groupe APB Pro2025

    Groupe APB Pro is the professional branch of Groupe APB, offering energy renovation solutions tailored to co-ownership properties, industry, the service sector, agriculture and local authorities. The project covered the redesign of the brand identity and website to adapt communication to different professional profiles and to the specific challenges of each sector. The goal was to make it easier to identify the right solutions for each activity, while strengthening the clarity of the offer, brand credibility and the user experience.

  • 2M Energie2025

    2M Energie is a French company specialising in energy renovation for homeowners, offering solutions such as solar panel and heat pump installation. The project covered the creation of the visual identity and the design of the website mock-ups, with the aim of building a modern, accessible image that reflects the brand’s expertise. The design sets out to inspire trust, highlight innovation and showcase personalised support for homeowners throughout their energy renovation projects.
