2M Energie

  • Branding
  • UI

2M Energie is a French company specialising in energy renovation for homeowners, offering solutions such as solar panel and heat pump installation.

The project covered the creation of the visual identity and the design of the website mock-ups, with the aim of building a modern, accessible image that reflects the brand’s expertise. The design sets out to inspire trust, highlight innovation and showcase personalised support for homeowners throughout their energy renovation projects.
