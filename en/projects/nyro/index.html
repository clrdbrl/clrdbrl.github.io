NYRO

  • Branding
  • UI

NYRO was born from a simple belief, beauty should be revealed, not masked. The project brings to life a natural skincare brand, healthy and effective, suited to every skin type and tone, with formulas grounded in dermatological research and clinically proven efficacy.

The visual identity expresses this standard through a clean, luminous universe. White and blue inspire trust, violet evokes the gentleness of care and red adds warmth. At the crossroads of medical rigour and sensitivity, it embodies an inclusive vision of natural beauty.
