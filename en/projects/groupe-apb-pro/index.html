Groupe APB Pro

  • Branding
  • UX
  • UI

Groupe APB Pro is the professional branch of Groupe APB, offering energy renovation solutions tailored to co-ownership properties, industry, the service sector, agriculture and local authorities.

The project covered the redesign of the brand identity and website to adapt communication to different professional profiles and to the specific challenges of each sector. The goal was to make it easier to identify the right solutions for each activity, while strengthening the clarity of the offer, brand credibility and the user experience.
